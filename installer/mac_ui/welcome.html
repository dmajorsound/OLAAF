olAAF – AAF Import for Reaper

Do you wanna build a Reaper Session?

olAAF imports AAF files from Pro Tools, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere and Avid into REAPER, with clips, fades, crossfades, clip gain and automation.

It is installed into your REAPER UserPlugins folder. Restart REAPER afterwards and find it under Extensions → olAAF.

Free for students. Developed by dmajorsound.
